The story so far

Shashi started posting outfit-of-the-day videos from her hostel room in her second year of college — mostly to kill time between lectures. Four years later, that same honest, unpolished style is exactly what brands come to her for.

She doesn't do overly staged content. Her audience follows her for the same reason a friend would recommend a product over coffee — because it feels true. That's also why she's one of the most requested creators in our lifestyle and beauty roster.

Her collaboration with Plōm Goodness — a week-long, unscripted "day in my routine" series — became one of the brand's best-performing pieces of content that quarter, without ever feeling like an ad.
