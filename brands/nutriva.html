The campaign

Ahead of a national retail launch, Nutriva needed recipe-led content that could make a new product feel familiar fast. We briefed 18 creators across food and fitness niches with a simple constraint — cook with it on camera, no scripts.

The recipe-led seeding built genuine anticipation ahead of shelf date, with several creator recipes still driving traffic to the brand's site months after launch.
