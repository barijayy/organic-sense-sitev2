The campaign

Plōm came to us ahead of a national skincare launch with a simple ask: make it feel like a recommendation, not an announcement. We built a 28-creator seeding wave across beauty and lifestyle audiences, briefed loosely enough that every video felt native to its creator's usual content.

The result was 24.8M reach and a 7.2% average engagement rate — well above the category norm — with several pieces of creator content later repurposed into the brand's own paid ad library.
